Loo finder application using GPS and Geomapping technology.

The video illustrates the concept, workflow and business plan.

It was a class room project from the course NTADBM 2011 where IIM & NID had collaborated.

Flush is a toilet finder mobile application, a service that helps you find the nearest public toilet. The application was born from insight of daily problems of people in urban India. There is general lack of proper sanitation and availability in most public places in India. There is lack of knowledge about good public toilets, most places there are no proper signage's for toilets. India interestingly has varied range of toilet options: western, Indian, urinal, compost pits, etc.

It’s a GPS based app, which narrows down where you are with just one click and gives the address of toilets within a given radius based upon geomapping. The service is available on smart phones through application and other phones through SMS service.

The application can be downloaded from the internet and registered users can rate each location on a five-star scale and give comments and reviews. Users can also add new locations from their mobile device, take photos of the facilities from their phone's camera and upload it directly to the online portal. Search options for specific queries can be made like Indian style or western, paid or unpaid, type of location (mall, restaurant, etc) and availability of handicap facilities.

The application is mainly targeted at women and the tourism sector. The navigation and the GUI of the app has been designed keeping specifically to this target group.
