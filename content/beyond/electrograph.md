It intends to display the consumption of electricity in real time and make the larger implications of wastage communicated in an easy, tangible & visual manner.

The video illustrates the concept via an initial prototype.

Our larger interest in communication & information led us to developing concepts for addressing ‘acts’ of communication and ‘works’ of communication to make people connected & aware. Electricity is one of the most important entities in our daily lives; as well as in the overall functioning of a country. In the recent past, India’s power consumption has been rapidly increasing, due to steady economic growth and a burgeoning population. If we were made aware of the consequences of using and wasting electricity on our natural environment, we would probably be motivated and informed enough to be more conscientious. The project is placed within the domain of visualisation, environmental issues, sustainability and surveillance of human activity. Combined with it is a tangible interface through which a display of human interactions and responses is possible. It intends to display the consumption of electricity in real time and make the larger implications of wastage communicated in an easy, tangible and visual manner.

We constructed a structure that reminds one of a spice cabinet to house the various components. Solenoid valves were used to control the amount of sand falling into the measuring jars. Each solenoid could be connected to the lights, fans or plug point switches. If a house has a digital meter with abilities to transfer data over a wireless home network, this data could be fed to the electrograph, showing the user in real time her consumption patterns. It has more of an impact than just looking at a bundle of numbers on a digital meter because one can see the tangible change in the level of sand, and how much a certain amount of electricity costs in terms of the environment.

Future direction, refining to make it smaller & minimalistic for household purposes.
