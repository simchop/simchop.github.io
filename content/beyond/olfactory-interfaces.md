It was an Interaction Design classroom project. The beginning was food which lead to exploration of olfactory interfaces, with development of a few interfaces. Few of the interfaces were smell books that could give off smells related to characters or chapters to enhance reading experience, a swearing meter that gives out bad smell when you swear, disc burner that gives a burning smell when your disc is ready, regulating air quality in smoking zones. The project progressed to research upon Anosmia. I found that tar can have similar effect on smokers and decided to investigate this phenomenon further. And [CigCheck](../cigcheck/) was the outcome of this investigation.
