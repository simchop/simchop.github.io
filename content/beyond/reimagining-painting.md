Remediation of the painting palette, trying to incorporate the real life painting experience on Tablets through the advanced technological capabilities of the medium.

The video illustrates the concept, workflow and interactions via a set of partially functional prototypes.

With immediacy as the underlying concept of technology now a day, the Tablets emerged to fulfil this need in our daily life. It is evolving and aims to become a technological and cultural artefact. The design brief by Adobe was to look at redefining the tools in an already existing desktop based format to that of a tablet. People are using the Tablet to create illustrations, artworks, graphics and presentations. I wanted to enhance the drawing experience on tablets by using the capabilities of the medium like built in sensors, touch, etc. The thought process lay in the domain of creating new tools, exploiting the additional capabilities of the medium, optimizing interactions with a natural and engaging experience..... Why not remediate the old, which led to thinking of an experience which was an amalgamation of real and virtual.

Remediation of the painting palette tries to incorporate the real life painting experience into the digital world. It provides capabilities of the touch for direct manipulation of paint and familiar painting gestures giving the user natural immersion. Simple design principles were also kept in mind while designing the interface. The mixing area of the palette can manipulate color, alpha and consistency. Consistency and alpha are interdependent in the real painting world but can be separated in the digital environment. With a natural simple finger rotation gesture, like a brush on a palette the consistency and alpha can be changed. The palette also uses the capabilities of the gyroscope in the tablet to further enhance the experience of painting on tablets.
